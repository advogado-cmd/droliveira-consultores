In October 2024 the Supreme Court kept suspended the rule that allowed pharmacists to perform invasive aesthetic procedures. In March 2026 the TRF-1 annulled the biomedical council's rule to the same effect, with nationwide reach. In August 2026 the same court declared illegal the CFO rule on orofacial harmonisation by dentists; the council appealed and the rule technically remains in force. For the clinic, this is not a legal debate. It is business design.

## What changes in practice

Clinics run by non-physicians need a medical technical officer for invasive procedures, a reviewed portfolio and franchise contracts re-read. Medical clinics gain a competitive advantage, including in advertising, with before-and-after images allowed by the CFM under rules.

## The wrong classification is expensive

A clinic registered as a beauty business (code 9602) performing medical procedures is fined by sanitary inspection and left unprotected in any lawsuit. Classification as a medical clinic requires a specific sanitary licence and a technical officer.

## What to do before next month

List every procedure in the portfolio with who performs it and under what authority. Where the answer is uncertain, the procedure leaves the schedule until the structure exists. It is uncomfortable for weeks and avoids a shutdown for months.
