Brazil's diagnostic sector passed one billion tests in 2024, with revenue at Abramed members growing 28.6%. At the same time, payers verticalise, cut their networks and buy laboratories. For the mid-sized laboratory, the question is whether it consolidates now, with value, or later, without a choice.

## Three measurements that decide

Concentration: share of revenue in the three largest payers. Term: average days to collect per payer. Denials: per payer, not in total. With the three, you know how much revenue is exposed to a single third-party decision.

## Compliance that is worth money

RDC 786/2023 replaced the operating rule for clinical laboratories; documented internal and external quality control is what a buyer checks first. Genetic data is sensitive data: the data authority prioritised health in 2026, and incidents must be notified within three business days.

## Health-techs selling to hospitals

Software that supports clinical decisions may be a medical device (RDC 657/2022) and require registration. Without it and without adherence to RDC 36, the hospital purchase does not close, however good the product.

## What to do

Measure the three indicators, fix compliance and only then decide. A laboratory that comes to the table with known dependence and demonstrated compliance negotiates; one that comes without them accepts.

*Sources: Abramed (Aug 2025); CBDL; Medicina S/A on the ANPD (2026).*
