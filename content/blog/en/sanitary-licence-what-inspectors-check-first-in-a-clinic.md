A sanitary inspection does not start in the procedure room. It starts with paper: the company registration, the corporate purpose, the council registration and the previous licence. If the activity code does not match what the clinic does, the visit changes tone before the inspector walks down the corridor.

## The sequence inspectors follow

First, the declared activity: activity code and corporate purpose must match the service provided. Second, technical responsibility: a current annotation with the professional council. Third, the approved building plan, where the activity requires it (RDC 50/2002 for healthcare establishments). Fourth, good operating practices (RDC 63/2011): protocols, risk management, maintenance. Only then come the details of each room.

## What one wrong item blocks

Without the right activity code there is no licence for the real activity, and without a licence the insurance policy stops covering what the clinic actually does. Without a technical officer, no licence is issued. Without a waste plan, the inspection issues a fine even when everything else is in order. That is why we treat licensing as a structural front, not as paperwork: each document holds up another.

## In São Paulo

The municipal licence is a prerequisite for operating. Renovations and expansions require an approved plan before the works; doing it the other way round is expensive.

## What to do this week

Print the company registration and compare it with the services on the clinic's website. If they differ, the first step of the plan is already defined.

*References: Anvisa RDC 50/2002 and 63/2011; São Paulo Ordinance SMS 266/2025. Confirm the rules are in force before deciding.*
