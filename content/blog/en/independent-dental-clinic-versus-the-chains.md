Dental chains with hundreds of units and private-equity backing have arrived in neighbourhoods with price, marketing and extended hours. Franchises in health, beauty and wellness grew 16.5% in 2024. The independent clinic that tries to imitate them loses; the one that positions itself grows.

## Three fronts where the independent clinic wins

Continuity: the same professional follows the patient for years, and that is worth more than a discount. Specialty: a service the standardised chain does not offer, or offers poorly. Local reputation: recent, real reviews, from patients in the same area.

## Where the clinic is exposed

Orofacial harmonisation: the CFO rule that allowed dentists to perform it was declared illegal in 2026 and is under appeal. A portfolio that depends on it needs a plan B now. Registration: corporate purpose, activity code, sanitary and radiology licences must match the real service. It is the first item a buying chain or the inspector checks.

## Paid traffic is not a strategy

Buying clicks solves the month and creates dependence. A website that schedules, a reactivated patient base and a differential stated in one sentence cost less and last longer.

## Where to start

Share of patients active in the last twelve months, average ticket and return rate. The plan starts with the worst of the three.
